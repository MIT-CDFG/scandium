A free, open‑source image editor that stays out of your way.

Layers, masks and adjustment layers. Retouching, selections, filters. A headless engine you can script. Nothing leaves your machine — no network, no telemetry, no account.

Built in the open

65 days. 1 high schooler. A fleet of agents.

The scripting door

The same engine, headless.

Every edit — in the app or from a shell — is one JSON command through one entry. This transcript is an excerpt of a real run: nothing in it is edited.
